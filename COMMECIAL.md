## When You Need a Commercial License

A commercial license is available for organizations that cannot or do not want to comply with the AGPL's copyleft obligations. Typical reasons include:

- You want to embed or distribute this software as part of a closed-source product or service.
- You run a hosted or internal service built on modified versions and cannot release the modifications.
- Your legal or compliance team does not permit AGPL-licensed dependencies.
- You need to keep your modifications proprietary.

A commercial license removes the copyleft obligations of the AGPL, allowing you to use the software without disclosing your source code[reference:4].

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need a commercial license?

The AGPL-3.0 license is sufficient for personal use and open-source projects. You need a commercial license if you plan to use the software in commercial products for sale, provide services in SaaS or cloud offerings, or use it in closed-source form and wish to keep your code private.

### What support is included with a commercial license?

Commercial licenses may include email support, bug fixes and patches, assistance with integration, priority for feature requests, and technical consultation, depending on the terms.

### Can I modify the software and use it commercially?

Under the AGPL-3.0, any modifications to the software must also be released under the AGPL-3.0. A commercial license allows you to keep your modifications proprietary.
